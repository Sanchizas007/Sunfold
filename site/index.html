Sunfold: Intermittent Fasting

16:8 timer, widget & phases

No account, no feed, no ads — just one ring and your own pace. A fasting timer that lives on your Lock Screen and keeps your data on your phone.

Download on the App Store

Sunfold is a fasting timer for people who would rather fast than scroll.

One ring shows how far you are. Start it when you finish your last meal, put the phone away, and come back whenever you like — the count does not drift, even after a restart.

ALONG THE WAY

Five phases, from the fed state to autophagy, each explained in plain language and honest about the hours being approximations, not measurements. 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, 5:2, or any length from 4 to 48 hours — change it mid-fast and the goal moves with you. A calendar of streaks and averages, with a note on how each fast felt. And weight, only if you want it: a quiet trend line, with no goal weight, no BMI and no verdicts.

ON YOUR PHONE, NOT IN YOUR WAY

The timer sits on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a Live Activity, and keeps counting without draining the battery. Reminders arrive when a fast is complete, half an hour before, and when your eating window closes — all scheduled on your iPhone, with no push server.

WHAT SUNFOLD LEAVES OUT

No account, no sign-in, no feed, no coach, no calorie counting, no ads. What you record stays on your device; export it as CSV or erase it in one tap. The only network traffic is to RevenueCat, to check whether Pro is active, and one anonymous Apple Ads attribution token per install.

SUNFOLD PRO

The 16:8 schedule and your last seven days are always yours. Pro adds every schedule, unlimited history, charts, the full phase detail and the widgets. Choose monthly, yearly or a single payment; a new subscription starts with a three-day free trial.

IMPORTANT

Sunfold is a timer and a journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before fasting if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, living with diabetes or taking medication that affects blood sugar, taking medicine that must be taken with food, underweight, or living with a chronic condition. If you have or have had an eating disorder, please seek support before using a fasting app.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, and can be managed in App Store settings at any time.
