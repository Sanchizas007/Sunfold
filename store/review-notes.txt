Sunfold is an offline intermittent-fasting timer for iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours, so no demo credentials are needed.

SEEING EVERYTHING IN TWO MINUTES
1. Open the app and go through the four welcome pages. The second is a health notice that has to be acknowledged before continuing.
2. A new install meets the Sunfold Pro paywall once, right after the welcome pages. Close it with the Close button, or choose a plan and tap Start free trial to test in the sandbox.
3. On the Timer tab tap Start fast, then the pencil on the Started card and move the start back thirteen hours. The ring, the metabolic phase pill and the History tab populate at once, so no demo data is needed. Tap the phase pill to read the five phases.
4. Settings has the full Privacy Policy and the Health notice. The Language row switches the interface between fifteen languages in place.

THE PAYWALL AND THE TRIAL
The monthly and yearly subscriptions carry a three-day free trial (a StoreKit introductory offer). Eligibility is checked for each customer: someone who has subscribed before is not shown the trial. Above the purchase button the paywall states the trial length, the price it turns into, the renewal and cancellation terms, with Restore, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Manage Subscription links beneath. A lifetime purchase is offered too. Installs from 1.1 and earlier keep the seven days of open access they were promised; that length is recorded per install and an update never shortens it.

WHAT CHANGED IN 1.3
The one functional change is Apple Ads attribution. Once per install the RevenueCat SDK passes Apple's AdServices attribution token to RevenueCat so we can tell whether an install came from an Apple Ads campaign. The token holds no personal data, is not linked to the user and is not used for tracking: no advertising identifier is read, the app never requests tracking authorisation, and there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The App Privacy answers now list Advertising Data (not linked to the user, not used for tracking), and the policy inside the app and on the website has a new Apple Ads attribution section. The store listing was rewritten in fifteen languages.

DATA AND NETWORK
Fasts, weight entries and notes stay on the device (an App Group shared with the widget) and are never transmitted. The only network traffic is RevenueCat purchase validation and the attribution token above. Reminders are scheduled locally on the phone.

HEALTH
Sunfold is a timer and a journal, not a medical device. The metabolic phase texts are general educational information, labelled as approximations; the app gives no diagnosis, treatment or personal advice and reads no health data from the device. A health notice is shown at first launch, in Settings and in the store description.
